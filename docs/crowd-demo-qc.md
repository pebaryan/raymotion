## 1. One-sentence conceit

> Four bodies, one set of rules: the same steering moves a plaza crowd round a fountain, a
> herd away from a dog, a flock of ducks through a banked turn and a school of fish round a
> pike, and one overlay per beat shows the rule as it acts.

Reviewer's restatement after viewing: two streams of people part round a fountain and each
other; a herd bolts as one from a running dog; a flock rolls into its turn; a school circles
a waiting pike, opens when it strikes, and closes again. One teal or amber overlay per beat
shows the rule at work, and each beat sounds like what it shows. Matches.

## 4. Critical fails

None. Known issues:

- **Sky band.** The environment's sky is a shade greyer than the paper at the top of
  beats 2 and 3.
- **Fleeing herd.** It strings out into a line (f300) rather than spreading.
- **Kicker crossed.** During the dive to the pond (about f460–470), distant plaza walkers
  pass behind `CROWDS`.
- **One fish at the rim.** At the height of the split (f540), one fish sits against the far
  rim's inner face.
- **Wingbeats.** They follow each duck's flap rate and fall silent on descents. The rig's
  seeded level-glide spells live inside `crowd::locomote` and are not mirrored, so some
  beats sound during glides. Twenty ducks blur into one flutter, so this is unlikely to be
  heard.
- **Score cache.** `ensureAssets` keeps an existing `score.wav`, as The Trackway does, and
  `still` runs it too. After any change to the world or the sound, delete
  `out/crowd-demo-assets/score.wav`; a stale score was caught once this session (section 7).
