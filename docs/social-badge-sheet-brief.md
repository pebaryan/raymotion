## 1b. Mode contract

A utility sheet succeeds when each of the 19 registered social and marketplace badge prefabs is visible, consistently sized, labeled by its public prefab name, and easy to compare. The layout is the proof object. Text is limited to a title, count, and labels. Packaging is a single PNG.

## 1. One sentence

This is a sheet of raymotion's social badges that lets a user compare every registered brand at a glance by placing the actual prefabs on one consistent grid.

## 2. One-line argument

Shopee, Tokopedia, Amazon, eBay, willhaben and Vinted fit the existing badge system alongside the earlier social and gaming platforms.

## 7. Audio plan

## 7b. Scenario coherence

The count in the title comes from `icons::brands().size()`. Each cell is built from its matching `social-` prefab name and `icons::title()` label, so labels cannot drift from the registered set.

## 8. Asset plan

The PNG is generated by the CLI still command. Glyphs are compiled into the engine; fonts use the engine fallback. No external runtime assets are required beyond the repository's normal font support. `list` remains functional from any directory.
